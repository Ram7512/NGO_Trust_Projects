Home Programs
01 / COMMUNITY CARE

Clothes Donation

SANP supports underprivileged families by providing essential clothing to people who need assistance. Through clothing donation initiatives, we aim to bring comfort, dignity and confidence to individuals and families facing difficult circumstances.

These initiatives encourage community participation and promote the spirit of sharing and caring for others.

02 / LEARNING OPPORTUNITIES

Books Donation

Education creates opportunities for children to build a better future. SANP encourages learning by providing books and essential educational materials to students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds.

Our efforts aim to reduce learning barriers, encourage reading habits and help children continue their educational journey with confidence.

03 / CHILD DEVELOPMENT

Education Support

SANP works to improve educational opportunities for children and young people in rural and marginalized communities. We encourage school participation, learning activities and access to educational resources.

By supporting students and encouraging their potential, we aim to create an environment where every child gets an opportunity to learn, grow and achieve their goals.

04 / HEALTH & WELLBEING

Health Support

Good health is essential for a strong and healthy community. SANP promotes health awareness, hygiene practices and access to basic healthcare support among rural and vulnerable communities.

Through awareness initiatives and community-based activities, we encourage people to adopt healthier lifestyles and take greater responsibility for their overall wellbeing.
