WHO WE ARE

Building Stronger Communities Through Collective Action.

Social Action For Needy People (SANP) is a registered voluntary organization working with marginalized and rural communities since 1987.

Our work combines community participation, institutional strengthening and resource mobilization to promote livelihoods, education, health, women empowerment, agriculture and watershed development. We focus on creating sustainable opportunities by empowering individuals and strengthening local communities. Through collaborative efforts, awareness programs and capacity-building initiatives, we encourage active participation and inclusive growth. Our goal is to build self-reliant communities where everyone has equal opportunities to improve their quality of life and contribute to a better future.

THE BEGINNING OF SANP

Every meaningful change begins with a commitment to make a difference.

Social Action For Needy People (SANP) began its journey in 1987 with a commitment to support people and strengthen communities.

Over the years, SANP has worked alongside women, farmers, youth and vulnerable families, encouraging community participation and creating opportunities for sustainable development.

From supporting Self Help Groups to promoting education, agriculture, health and livelihood opportunities, the organisation continues to work towards building self-reliant communities.

Real development happens when people become active participants in shaping their own future.

— SANP's Community Development Approach

WHAT DRIVES US

Our Mission

To empower marginalized groups through participatory development, strengthen women's SHGs and community institutions, and promote livelihoods, education, health, agriculture and resource development. We aim to build awareness, enhance local leadership and encourage active community participation in development activities. Through sustainable initiatives, capacity building and effective resource utilization, we strive to create lasting positive changes in the lives of disadvantaged communities.

WHAT WE ASPIRE TO

Our Vision

A self-reliant society where women, youth, farmers and vulnerable people live with dignity, participate in local decisions and create sustainable futures for their communities. We envision an inclusive and equitable society where everyone has equal opportunities to grow, access essential resources and improve their quality of life. Our vision is to build empowered communities that contribute to long-term social and economic development.

OUR APPROACH

Development Through People

SANP strengthens rural institutions, builds collective action and connects communities with government, banks and development partners for lasting results. It encourages active community participation, promotes inclusive decision-making and supports local leadership. Through capacity building, awareness programmes and sustainable development initiatives, SANP helps rural communities access essential resources, improve livelihoods and become more self-reliant. These efforts create stronger institutions and empower communities to achieve long-term social and economic progress.
