ABOUT SANP

Creating Self-Reliant Communities Through Participation, Leadership & Sustainable Development

35+

Years Of Service

Social Action For Needy People (SANP) is a non-governmental organization committed to empowering marginalized and rural communities through participatory development approaches. Since its inception, SANP has worked alongside women, farmers, youth and vulnerable families to promote sustainable livelihoods, strengthen local institutions and improve access to education, health and economic opportunities.

The organization believes that real development can only be achieved when communities actively participate in identifying their challenges and designing solutions. Through Self Help Groups (SHGs), community federations, agricultural development initiatives and leadership training programs, SANP has created long-lasting impact across villages.

Creating Opportunities For Long-Term Growth

Empowering women through collective action, leadership development and sustainable livelihood opportunities.

Over the years, SANP has worked with thousands of rural women across villages by supporting Self Help Groups (SHGs), strengthening local institutions and encouraging economic participation. Women involved in these programs gain access to training, savings mechanisms, financial services and livelihood opportunities that improve household income and community well-being.

Beyond economic empowerment, these initiatives help women become leaders within their villages, participate in community decision-making and contribute towards sustainable development. SANP continues to create environments where women can build confidence, share knowledge and work together to address local challenges.
