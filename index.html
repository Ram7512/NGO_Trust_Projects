ABOUT SANP

Empowering Communities Through Participation, Leadership And Sustainable Development

35+

Years Of Service

Social Action For Needy People (SANP) is a registered voluntary organization established in 1987 to support marginalized communities. We work alongside women, farmers, youth and vulnerable families in the areas of livelihoods, education, health, women empowerment and natural resource development.

Our approach strengthens community institutions and gives people practical tools to plan and manage their own development. Through SHGs, federations, training, advocacy and resource mobilization, SANP helps build resilient and self-reliant villages.

Creating Opportunities For Long-Term Growth

Empowering women through collective action, leadership development and sustainable livelihood opportunities.

Over the years, SANP has worked with thousands of rural women across villages by supporting Self Help Groups (SHGs), strengthening local institutions and encouraging economic participation. Women involved in these programs gain access to training, savings mechanisms, financial services and livelihood opportunities that improve household income and community well-being.

Beyond economic empowerment, these initiatives help women become leaders within their villages, participate in community decision-making and contribute towards sustainable development. SANP continues to create environments where women can build confidence, share knowledge and work together to address local challenges.
